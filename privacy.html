Gillu privacy policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Gillu is a homework helper for parents. A parent takes a photo of their child's homework and Gillu explains it to the parent. Gillu is made for adults. Children do not create accounts.

What we collect and why

WhatWhyKept?
Your Google account name, email address and profile picture linkTo sign you inYes, until you delete your account
Your child's class, school board and your chosen languageTo explain at the right levelYes, until you delete your account
The homework photo you sendTo read the question and write the explanationNo. It is processed and not stored on our servers. A copy stays on your own phone so you can see it again.
The explanation: subject, topic, the question text and the stepsTo count your daily free uses and to improve wrong explanationsYes, until you delete your account

We do not collect your child's name, your location, your contacts or advertising identifiers. Gillu shows no ads and we do not sell data.

Who helps us process it

  • Supabase stores accounts and saved explanations.
  • Mistral AI reads the homework photo, writes the explanation and makes the voice. The photo is sent to Mistral for this purpose only.
  • Google provides sign-in.

Please do not send photos that show faces or personal details. Photograph only the homework page.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Settings and tap Delete my data. This deletes your account and all saved explanations from our servers straight away, and clears the homework saved on your phone. If you cannot open the app, email us from the address you signed in with and we will delete it within 30 days.
